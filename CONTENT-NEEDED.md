# Content checklist

Details to collect for the site. Everything below is currently a `[placeholder]` in `index.html`
(open `index.html?todo` to see them all highlighted).

## The basics
- [ ] **Business name** (exactly how it should appear)
- [ ] **Owner name** (and how he wants to be introduced: full name, first name, "Mr. ___")
- [ ] **Phone number** (and is texting OK?)
- [ ] **Email address** for the site (where estimate requests should go)
- [ ] **City / service area** (main city plus nearby towns and counties he'll travel to)
- [ ] **Business hours**
- [ ] **How fast he replies** to new requests (e.g. "within 1 business day")

## Credentials
- [ ] **Years of experience**
- [ ] Rough **number of projects** completed
- [ ] **Contractor license number** (if licensed; many states require it to be shown)
- [ ] **Insured?** (liability insurance, and bonded if so)
- [ ] Any certifications or trade memberships
- [ ] **Languages spoken** (e.g. Spanish)

## Services: confirm or edit what's listed
**Floor tile:** porcelain, ceramic, natural stone, large-format, subfloor prep, old floor removal, patterns
- [ ] Anything to add (backsplashes? outdoor patios? wall tile? heated floors?) or remove?

**Bathrooms:** showers, tub surrounds, tub-to-shower conversions, waterproofing, niches and benches
- [ ] Does he do plumbing and fixtures himself, or tile and finish work only? Full remodels?

**Pools:** new builds, waterline tile, coping, decks, repair
- [ ] What exactly does he do for pools? Full builds, or tile, coping and decks? Repairs and resurfacing?

## Photos (the most important part)
- [ ] **10–20 photos of finished work**, the best ones, a mix of tile, bathrooms and pools
- [ ] **Before and after** pairs, if any
- [ ] A **photo of him** (on the job is great; a clear, friendly photo works too)
- [ ] For each photo: what it was and roughly where (e.g. "Master shower, Riverside")

Tips: horizontal photos, good daylight, clean site, no customers' faces or house numbers.

## About section
- [ ] How did he get started? How long has he been doing this?
- [ ] What is he proud of / what makes his work different?
- [ ] Why did he add pools?
- [ ] Family business? Anything personal he's comfortable sharing?

## Reviews
- [ ] 3–6 **real** reviews from past customers (Google, Facebook, Yelp, or text messages,
      shared with the customer's OK). First name and neighborhood is enough.
- [ ] Does he have a **Google Business Profile**? (Link to it from the site, which also helps him show up in local search.)

## Nice to have
- [ ] Logo (or keep the simple tile icon)
- [ ] Favorite colors, or a design direction from `mockups/`
- [ ] Social media links (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok)
- [ ] A domain name he'd like (e.g. `smithtileandpools.com`)
