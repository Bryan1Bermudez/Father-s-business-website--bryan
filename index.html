About

Meet Jacinto Bermudez

Jacinto has been setting tile professionally since 2003 and founded Millennium Flooring Remodeling in 2007. For more than twenty years he has installed tile floors and bathrooms, and supervised crews on residential and commercial remodeling projects, working as an owner, a supervisor, and a trusted subcontractor for local renovation and flooring companies.

Before construction, he earned a Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering in Cuba, and it shows: careful plan reading, accurate measurements, and high standards on every job. He is OSHA 30-hour certified and speaks Spanish and English. [Optional: a line about why he started building pools.]
